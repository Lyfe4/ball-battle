Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

BallBattle is made by Calvin Reinke, in New South Wales, Australia. This policy explains what the game collects when you play online, why, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. Questions or requests: calvin@calvinrdevelopment.com.

Playing offline

Playing against the bot, Free play and the ball manual send nothing about you to us. Your settings, a series in progress and which tips you've seen are kept only in your browser's storage, on your device, and clearing the site's data removes them.

Like any website, the game's pages come from our host, Netlify, whose servers see your IP address when they send them. The game also asks now and then whether a newer version is out; that request carries nothing about you.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase, a US company, stores the database in Sydney, Australia, and runs the guest sign-ins.
  • Netlify, a US company, hosts the site. The game's server code runs in Ohio, USA, so every online request passes through the United States on its way to the database in Sydney.

Both act only on our instructions. Their systems also keep short-lived technical logs, which can include your IP address. Our own server logs each online request with your guest ID (never your IP address or what you sent): which part of the game, when, and whether it worked. Netlify keeps these logs; they expire on its own schedule, and Forget me doesn't remove them, though the ID then belongs to no one. We use no other service for online play; if we add one, this page will say so first.

Deleting your data

Use Forget me in the game's settings, there on any device that has a guest, even while online play is switched off. It deletes your guest straight away: your guest ID, player name, age confirmation, sign-in session with the IP address recorded on it, and your challenge links. A match still being played is forfeited to your opponent. Finished matches stay in your opponent's history as “Deleted player”, no longer linked to you. It also clears the game's data from that browser.

A guest nobody has used for 90 days is deleted the same way. If you clear your browser's data, your guest can't be reached from it any more and is deleted after those 90 days. Copies in our providers' logs and backups expire on their own schedule.

You can also email us to ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted: tell us your player name and roughly when you played.

Children

Online play is for people aged 16 and over. If you believe a younger person has used it, email us and we'll delete their data.

Complaints

Please contact us first. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) or, in Europe or the UK, your local data protection authority.
